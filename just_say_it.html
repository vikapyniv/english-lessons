Pattern unlocked
So what is the core difference?
SPEAKLanguages, more formal communication, public speaking, or the act/ability of speaking.
TALKConversation and interaction, usually more informal and often about a topic.
SAYFocus on the words/message. A listener is optional; if named, use say to someone.
TELLFocus on giving information to a person. It normally needs a listener: tell someone something.
