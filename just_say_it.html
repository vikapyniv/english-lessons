01 · Video

Watch and listen

Watch each clip, discuss the two questions, then try the mini-quiz.

Who am I speaking to?

  1. How do the speakers ask who is on the other end of the call?
  2. Which version sounds more formal to you, and why?

Which is a standard polite way to ask for someone on the phone?

You’re not telling me everything

  1. What does the speaker think the other person is hiding?
  2. Who is the listener in “You’re not telling me everything”?

Please ___ me what happened.

Are you saying that …?

  1. Why does the speaker start with “Are you saying that …?”
  2. What idea does the speaker want to check in one of the scenes?

Did she ___ that the meeting was cancelled?

What are you talking about?

  1. What are the characters discussing in one of the scenes?
  2. Does the speaker sound confused, surprised, or annoyed? What makes you think so?

Which question is more typical in an everyday conversation?
