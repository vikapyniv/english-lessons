3. This person prefers to double-check that they put all the things they need in a bag.
4. This person starts arranging a trip very early.

Work in pairs.

Choose one picture together and read it. Discuss how you would prepare and what you would do, using vocabulary from the lesson. Then each student asks their partner one extra question about the situation. Answer, then move to the next picture.

What to include

Say when you will start preparing for the trip and what you will do:

  1. a month before the trip
  2. a week before the trip
  3. a day before the trip
  4. on the day of the trip
