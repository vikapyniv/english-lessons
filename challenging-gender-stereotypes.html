07 Read and discuss

Read the texts about women from the past who challenged stereotypes and choose one option to complete each sentence.

Marie Curie

Marie Curie (1867–1934), known for her discovery of radioactivity, was the first woman to win the Nobel Prize for her work in two different scientific fields, Physics and Chemistry. Curie loved science and was to become a scientist and do something important. At a time when women weren’t welcome in scientific fields, things weren’t easy for her but she was enough to challenge social expectations as well as to face big economic difficulties. Her spirit led her to explore new ideas and make important discoveries, inspiring future generations of women to go to university and have scientific careers.

Amelia Earhart

Among her many aviation records, Amelia Earhart (1897–1937) became famous mainly for being the first female pilot to fly solo across the Atlantic Ocean. While some people thought journalists were being about her adventures only to attract public interest, for many others she became a role model. She used her fame to inspire other women to challenge gender stereotypes in male-dominated fields, particularly aviation. Earhart said this solo flight showed men and women were equal, which led many to call her crazy or even . Earhart dreamed of achieving even greater things. Although she was treated as by many for what seemed to be an impossible dream, she set out to fly around the globe, a journey that sadly led to her disappearance.
