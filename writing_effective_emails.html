Bad email

Subject: SOS!!! ANSWER NOW

GreetingHello Aleksandra,
Paragraph 1 · PurposeI am so sorry but I need your help.
Paragraph 2 · ExplanationI want to tell you that I cannot see the lessons on our platform. I sent you a screenshot below.
Paragraph 3 · Additional informationI also want to ask for information about my lessons next week because I do not get any schedule.
Paragraph 4 · Request for actionPlease check if everything is OK and answer me now because this is very urgent.
Closing and sign-offThank you.
See you soon,
Bye,
Vika

Professional email

Subject: Unable to Access the Platform and Lesson Schedule

GreetingDear Aleksandra,
Paragraph 1 · PurposeI am writing to request your assistance with a problem on the teaching platform.
Paragraph 2 · ExplanationI would like to inform you that I cannot see my lessons on the platform. I have attached a screenshot of the issue below.
Paragraph 3 · Additional informationI would also like to request information about my lessons for next week because I have not received a schedule.
Paragraph 4 · Request for actionCould you please verify whether everything is working correctly?
Closing and sign-offThank you for your time and assistance. I look forward to your reply.

Kind regards,
Viktoriia Pyniv
Reveal the main differences

The professional version uses a descriptive subject line, a more appropriate greeting, formal verbs, present perfect for the missing schedule, a polite indirect request, and one consistent sign-off.
