Useful vocabulary

  • Store credit — money a shop gives you when you return something or as a reward; you can only spend it in that shop.
  • Hardware store (AmE) — a DIY shop (BrE); a shop selling tools and materials for repairs.
  • Wrench (AmE) — a spanner (BrE); a tool used to tighten or loosen nuts and bolts.
  • Purse (AmE) — a handbag (BrE); a bag used to carry personal belongings.
  • Coupon (AmE) — a voucher (BrE); something that gives you a discount or special offer.
  • Math (AmE) — maths (BrE); mathematics.
Discuss the Questions
  1. Which excuse from Raccoon or Kitty could you actually imagine yourself using? Would you admit it out loud?
  2. Imagine your friend spends £300 on something completely unnecessary and calls it an “investment”. Would you challenge them or help them justify it?
  3. What’s the most ridiculous thing you’ve ever bought just because it was on sale? Did you regret it?
  4. Is buying something expensive ever smarter than choosing the cheaper option? Where do you draw the line between a smart investment and self-deception?
  5. Who’s easier to fool with a discount: someone who loves shopping or someone who thinks they’re very good with money? Defend your answer.
