Section 1

Life in large cities

Connect personal experience with major cities around the world, then examine the most common urban problems.

1

Choose three cities.

Select exactly three city cards, then discuss them using the questions on the right.

0 of 3 cities selected

If not, what would you expect?What would you want to see, and do you think you would enjoy living there?

Which problems would make you not want to move to a city?
