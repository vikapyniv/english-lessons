Final discussion

Reflect & discuss

Discuss the questions with a partner. Use as much target vocabulary from the lesson as possible.

1. Which words from today’s lesson were completely new to you?
2. Which words were familiar, but you had never used them in these combinations before?
3. What is the difference between a bottle, a carton, a jar and a can?
4. Why do we say a slice of bread but a piece of cake? Can you think of other examples?
5. What is the difference between a box, a packet and a bag? What products can come in each one?
6. Which containers are mainly used for liquids? Give as many examples as you can.
7. Which containers are usually made of glass, plastic, cardboard or metal?
8. Why do you think English uses words like slice, piece, bar and tub instead of just naming the food?
9. Complete the collocation: a bunch of grapes, a bunch of flowers, a bunch of keys… What else can we use a bunch of with?
10. What is the difference between a tin of tomatoes and a can of tomatoes? Which one would you normally use?
