Discuss the questions
  1. Why do you think UNPAID CARE WORK (care without pay) is often TAKEN FOR GRANTED (not appreciated)?
  2. What skills can people GAIN (get / develop) through CAREGIVING (looking after someone)?
  3. How can a workplace be more ACCOMMODATING (flexible and helpful) to employees with caring responsibilities?
  4. When should an employee be EXPLICIT ABOUT (very clear about) their needs at work?
  5. Should caregiving experience be included on a CV (résumé)? Why or why not?
  6. Why might caregivers be PASSED OVER FOR (not chosen for) key projects or promotions?
  7. What kind of FLEXIBILITY (more choice / freedom) should employers offer to caregivers?
  8. When is it reasonable to TAKE TIME OFF (stop work temporarily) to care for someone?
  9. What BOUNDARIES (personal limits) should employees be able to set around their family responsibilities?
  10. Could projects based on MILESTONES (important stages) and DELIVERABLES (finished work / results) reduce the need to RELY ON FREQUENT (depend on many regular) meetings and CHECK-INS (short status meetings)? Why?
