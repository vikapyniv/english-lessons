The right age to get a smartphone

1. How old should kids be to get phones?

Read the main idea from the first part of the article.

How old should kids be to get phones?

A 2019 report found that by age 11, more than half of children in the U.S. had their own smartphone. By 12, more than two-thirds did, and by 14, most teenagers had one. Nearly 20% of 8-year-olds already had their own smartphone.

Still, there is no single “right” age. The best time depends on whether both the child and the parents feel that the child is ready.

Discuss the questions
  1. How old were you when you got your first phone, and do you think that was the right age for you?
  2. Do you think children today need smartphones earlier than children did in the past? Why or why not?
  3. Is it reasonable for parents to forbid a child from having a smartphone? In what situations?
  4. What has changed most between childhood now and childhood before smartphones became common?
  5. Why do you think people care so much about finding the “right age” for a first smartphone?
