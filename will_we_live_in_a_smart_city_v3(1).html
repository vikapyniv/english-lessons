2. Mobility Hub Survey

Imagine the government wants to introduce mobility hubs in your city or town, but you must do a survey first.
Choose the answer that is closest to your opinion. Then compare your choices with a partner and explain why.
Future Simple — prediction/opinion

1. How do you think mobility hubs will change daily life in your city?

Present Simple — timetable/schedule

2. Which timetable would work best for you?

Be going to — plan/intention

3. Which change are you most likely to make if the hubs open?

Present Continuous — arranged future

4. The city is holding events about the new hubs next week. Which arrangement suits you best?
