02

Four ways to face your fears

Discuss each therapy. Do you think it is effective? Could it help you or other people? Does it make sense?

↗

Exposure Therapy

Face what scares you in a safe setting, starting with small steps and gradually moving to more difficult situations.

Little by little
◉

Flooding Therapy

Face a very intense version of your fear for a longer time instead of increasing exposure gradually.

All at once
✦

Mindfulness

Notice your fear without panicking. Slow down, breathe deeply and observe your thoughts and feelings.

Notice, don’t panic
◇

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT)

Talk about the thoughts behind your fear, challenge irrational beliefs and face difficult situations with support.

Change thought patterns
