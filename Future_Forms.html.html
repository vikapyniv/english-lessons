Future Forms: Plans & Arrangements

A Busy Weekend Ahead

Maya has a busy weekend ahead. On Saturday morning, her train leaves at 8:15, so she needs to get up early. She is meeting her friend Leo at the station at 7:45, and they already have their tickets.

They are going to spend the day in Brighton because they want to relax by the sea. They haven't planned every detail yet, but Maya wants to visit the pier and try a new restaurant.

Just before leaving home, Leo calls and says he has forgotten to bring a charger. Maya looks at the one on her desk and says, “No problem, I'll bring mine.”

Discuss

  1. Do you usually plan your weekends in advance, or do you prefer to decide at the last minute?
  2. Have you ever had a plan change because someone forgot something or something unexpected happened?
  3. If you could plan a perfect weekend away right now, where would you go and what would you do?
