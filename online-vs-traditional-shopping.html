Task 4 · Discussion

Let's talk about shopping trends

Discuss the questions with your partner. Share your opinions and personal experiences, give detailed examples, and ask follow-up questions. Use at least TWO highlighted target expressions in EACH answer.

01

Imagine you are watching a live-stream event. What would persuade you to place an order immediately, and what would make you leave?

Useful language: live-stream event · place an order
02

How can online retailers showcase products without making customers feel pressured to buy? Think of a really convincing example.

Useful language: retailers · showcase
03

Why has livestream shopping gained popularity in some countries? What new shopping trend could take off where you live?

Useful language: gain popularity · take off
04

Would you buy fresh produce online, or would you rather visit a shop and choose it yourself? What could change your mind?

Useful language: fresh produce · visit a shop · change your mind
05

Have you ever jumped on the bandwagon and bought something just because everyone else had it? Did it turn out to be a bargain or a waste of money?

Useful language: jump on the bandwagon · bargain
06

If you became a creator, which products would you feature in your videos? How would you help viewers shop around before buying?

Useful language: feature products · shop around
07

Why might some shoppers feel lukewarm about livestream shopping? What would make YOU more interested in trying it?

Useful language: lukewarm · live-stream shopping
08

Do big discounts encourage people to spend too much money? Tell your partner about a sale you were tempted by.

Useful language: discounts · spend money
09

What product or shopping app has taken your country by storm? Why did it become so popular, and do you think it will last?

Useful language: take something by storm · gain popularity
10

After buying something online, have you ever needed to make a complaint or get a full refund? What happened, and how did the retailer respond?

Useful language: make a complaint · get a full refund
