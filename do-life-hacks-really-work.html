4. Solve the problems with a life hack.

For each problem, explain a life hack that could solve it. You can use the suggested idea or invent your own. You must use the two target words in your answer.

EXAMPLE · Gum stuck to a carpet
Possible life hack: carpet + ice cubes or invent your own
Must use: place · remove
Example answer: Place some ice cubes on the gum and leave them there for about 30 minutes. When the gum becomes hard, remove it carefully from the carpet.

Useful action verbs & phrases

flip=turn over quickly
shake=move back and forth
open=make not closed
stick=push into / attach
place=put in position
turn=change direction / rotate
push=press away / forward
twist=rotate with force
put=move to a place
slide=move smoothly
squeeze=press firmly
remove=take something away
hold=keep in your hand
pull=move towards you
press=push down firmly
cut=divide with a blade
tie=fasten with string
hang=attach from above
rub=move against a surface
light=make start burning
