Things Go Better with Coca-Cola

Created by Viktoriia Pyniv

1. What do you know about Coca-Cola?

Choose one fact you think is true.

2. Read for the main idea

Read quickly. Which sentence best summarises the text?

Coca-Cola is enjoyed all over the world. Around 1.6 billion gallons are sold every year in more than 160 countries. The drink was invented by Dr John Pemberton in Atlanta in 1886, and it was given the name Coca-Cola by his partner Frank Robinson. The business was bought by Asa Candler in 1888, and the first factory was opened in Dallas in 1895. Billions of bottles have been produced since then, but the recipe is still kept secret.

4. Complete the facts

Write the correct passive form in each sentence.

Coca-Cola all over the world. (enjoy)

The drink in 1886. (invent)

The first factory in Dallas in 1895. (open)

Billions of bottles since 1895. (produce)

5. Talk about a product

Choose one everyday product and complete the prompts.

This product is made from / of …

It is produced in …

It is sold in …

It is used for …

Example: Coffee is made from coffee beans. It is sold in cafés and supermarkets.
