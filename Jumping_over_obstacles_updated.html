Quick grammar tip: prepositions of movement

THROUGH — Move through the inside of something, often with things around you or in your way.
Walk through a forest. Go through a tunnel.
ACROSS — Move from one side to the other across an area or surface (not through its inside).
Walk across a street. Swim across a lake.
OVER — Move above an obstacle to the other side.
Jump over a fence. Climb over a wall.
FORWARD — Move straight ahead; no destination is mentioned.
Take two steps forward. Keep moving forward.
TO — Move to a specific destination or endpoint.
Go to school. Walk to the station.
ALONG — Move following a path, road, river or coast.
Walk along the beach. Drive along the coast.
