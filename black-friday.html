2. Alternatives to but

Read the table and compare each sentence with its version using but. What do these linkers mean?

LinkerExamples
although / even though

I didn’t have any money, but I went on a Black Friday shopping spree.

Although / Even though I didn’t have any money, I went on a Black Friday shopping spree.

in spite of / despite

I didn’t have any money, but I went on a Black Friday shopping spree.

I went on a Black Friday shopping spree in spite of / despite not having any money.

I didn’t have any money, but I went on a Black Friday shopping spree.

I went on a Black Friday shopping spree in spite of / despite the fact that I didn’t have any money.

I didn’t have any money, but I went on a Black Friday shopping spree.

I went on a Black Friday shopping spree in spite of / despite the lack of money.

nevertheless

I didn’t have any money, but I went on a Black Friday shopping spree.

I didn’t have any money. Nevertheless, I went on a Black Friday shopping spree.

whereas / while

I went on a Black Friday shopping spree, but my friend decided to save some money this year and skipped Black Friday.

I went on a Black Friday shopping spree, whereas / while my friend decided to save some money this year and skipped Black Friday.

3. Speaking · Black Friday opinions

Look at the example. It gives two short ideas and shows how to connect them with a linker.

StatementIdea 1Contrasting idea
1. A small shop should offer big discounts to go in the black (= start earning more than it spends) because lower prices can bring in more customers. Big discounts bring in more customers. The shop earns less on each sale.

Example. Even though big discounts bring in more customers, a small shop might struggle to go in the black if it earns less on each sale.

Now do the same with the statements below. Use the reason in each statement as a starting point. Write it in your own words, then add a contrasting idea. Connect both ideas with a linker and discuss them. Say whether you agree and explain why. Ask your partner what they think and why. You can also give examples from your own life.

StatementIdea 1Contrasting idea
2. Black Friday sales should fall on (= happen on) one day only because shops could offer bigger discounts for one day than for a whole week.
3. It’s worth showing up (= arriving) early for a sale because the things you want might be gone by the time you get there.
4. The word “cheap” has a negative connotation (= feeling a word gives you) in clothing ads because people might think the clothes are poor quality.
5. A shop that’s already in the black (= earning more than it spends) should lower its prices because more people could afford to shop there.
6. If you’re in the red (= you owe money or spend more than you earn), you should wait before buying a new phone because it’s harder to pay off debt when you keep spending.
7. You’re more likely to get ripped off (= get tricked into paying too much) online because you can’t always tell what you’re getting before you pay.
8. It’s worth splashing out on (= spending a lot of money on) a good winter coat because you can wear it for years.
9. It’s OK to dip into your savings (= use some money you’ve saved) for a holiday because it can be hard to find a time when all your friends are free.
10. It’s worth paying through the nose (= pay far too much) to see your favourite band because they might not come to your city again for years.
11. It’s a good idea to cut back on (= spend less on) eating out because bringing lunch from home can save you money every week.
12. A savvy shopper (= someone who knows how to shop well) waits for clothes to go on sale because the same items often cost less a few weeks later.
13. It’s easy to get lured into buying (= be tempted to buy) things you don’t need because a countdown makes you feel like you have to decide right away.
14. It’s fine to go on a shopping spree (= buying lots of things in a short time) once a year because you can plan ahead and save up for it.
15. The Black Friday frenzy (= a time when lots of people rush to shop) makes shopping more fun because going out with friends and looking for deals can feel like a special event.
16. It’s fine for shops to put a positive spin on (= make something sound better) a small discount because even a small saving can help when you’re buying something expensive.
