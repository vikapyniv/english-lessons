Fear of the dentist

Discuss the questions
Do you find the advice below useful? Why/Why not? What other advice would you add?
  • bringing a friend or family member for support
  • telling the dentist what you are afraid of (e.g. needles, sounds, smells)
  • listening to music and podcasts or watching a video
  • booking appointments early in the morning to avoid worrying all day
  • deep breathing
  • using hand signals to ask for a break
  • asking people you know to recommend a good dentist
  • finding a dentist you trust
  • asking for special medication
  • rewarding yourself with something enjoyable after the visit
