Activity 04 · Video & speaking

An unlikely friendship

Watch the story and discuss the questions.

Discuss the questions

  1. What do you think about the story in the video?
  2. Do you have any friends who are much older or younger than you?
  3. What could be some advantages and disadvantages of having friends of different ages?
  4. Do you have any unlikely friendships? What makes them different? Why are you friends?
Activity 05 · Slides 34–35

Complete the four friendship stories

Read the model answers and type the missing phrasal verbs or particles. Each blank turns green when correct and red when incorrect.

AGwen & Tobias

Tobias can Gwen because she’s doing great at school, and he can ask her for advice on how to improve his grades. Tobias can Grace to spend time with him and his friends, and help her to make new friends.

BOwen & Klaudia

Klaudia can help Owen when he gets angry by talking to him and helping him relax while baking together. Owen can Klaudia by helping her bake cakes and cookies when she’s lonely.

CRuben & Maximus

Maximus can invite Ruben to play video games after work so that Ruben can relax. Maximus can Ruben for support when he his colleagues.

DHadeel & Rocio

Hadeel can Rocio and take her on an exciting adventure to help her try something new. Rocio can Hadeel with her finances by showing her how to create a budget and save money for a house.
