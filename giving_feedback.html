Language guide

I see your point, but…I understand, but I disagree.
Have you thought about…?Maybe consider this idea.
I’d rather not…I prefer not to do it.
What if you tried…?Why not try this?
I’d be open to that if…I can agree, but only with a condition.
An alternative would be to…Here is another option.
I’m a bit concerned that…I see a possible problem.
I’d suggest that…This is my recommendation.
It’s definitely worth…This idea is good to try.
Do you think it might help if…?Could this change make it better?
