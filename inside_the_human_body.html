Vocabulary

Explore the body, match key terms and practise using them.

1. Look at the body parts in the box and explain why humans need them.

bonesmusclesbrainskin heartstomachhipthroat

2. Place the organs on the human body.

Drag the mini pictures onto the silhouette. Put them where you think each organ is located.

3. Match the organ names with the pictures.

Click a word, then click the matching picture.

4. Write a short answer.

Write one to three words next to each question as a quick note for yourself.

5. Drag the body-system words into the sentences.

Drag the words into the correct sentences. When you finish, give two or three more examples of organs or body parts that belong to each body system.

Healthy or Harmful?

Discuss how everyday habits may affect different organs.

6. Work with your partner.

Choose a habit from the menu. Drag one or more organs into the columns and explain every choice.

  • You may place several organs in the same column.
  • You may use the same organ more than once.
  • Discuss why the effect may change depending on the amount, frequency or person.
  • Mark a habit as discussed before choosing another one.
There is no single correct answer. Your explanation is the most important part.
