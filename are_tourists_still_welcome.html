4 Language review

Find an incorrect word in each sentence and replace it with a correct one.

Write one corrected word in each gap. Check your answers, then discuss all the statements.

A. More and more people are feed up with the rising cost of living.
B. People often blame technology to problems that they cause themselves.
C. Young people today have to dial with more stress than previous generations.
D. Customers are more likely to complain with the product they bought than to leave positive feedback.
E. Artificial intelligence could go out of control in the future.
F. People who work from home often struggle against work-life balance.
G. Social media brings too much pressure on people to have a perfect life.
Answers: A fed; B for; C deal; D about; E get; F with; G puts.
