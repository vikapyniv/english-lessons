02

Reflexive pronouns

Read each set of four sentences from left to right. Complete the reflexive pronouns in the highlighted sentences.

I
I learned how to cook.
I taught myself how to cook.
I learned English without a teacher.
I learned English by myself.
You · singular
You learned to play the guitar.
You taught to play the guitar.
You went to the concert alone.
You went to the concert .
She
She learned to speak French.
She taught to speak French.
She travelled to Paris alone.
She travelled to Paris .
He
He repaired his bike instead of paying someone.
He repaired his bike .
He went hiking alone.
He went hiking .
It
The robot automatically switched off.
The robot switched off.
The robot moved without anyone controlling it.
The robot moved .
We
We painted the room instead of hiring painters.
We painted the room ourselves.
We painted the room without help.
We painted the room by ourselves.
You · plural
You all organised the event instead of hiring a planner.
You all organised the event .
You all went on holiday without anyone else.
You all went on holiday .
They
They built the shelves instead of hiring someone.
They built the shelves .
They went camping without anyone else.
They went camping .
