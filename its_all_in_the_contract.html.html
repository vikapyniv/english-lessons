2. Discuss the questions

  1. What are the advantages for both employers and employees of hiring people under a contract for services? Are there any disadvantages?
  2. Do you think permanent contracts are becoming less common? If yes, in what industries?
  3. Who benefits more from a fixed-term contract: the employee or the employer? Why?
  4. Do you think zero-hours contracts should be banned? Why/Why not?
  5. What consequences might there be for a breach of contract?
  6. Can you think of some situations when breaking an NDA could be justified?
  7. How ethical is it for a company to offer unpaid internships?
  8. Is it common for people to do an apprenticeship where you live? If yes, in which professions?
