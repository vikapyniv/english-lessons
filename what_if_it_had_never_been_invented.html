3

What is the reality?

Compare each conditional with the real situation. Notice how the verb forms show the time of the condition and result. Then complete the mini-quiz.

Past SimplePast Perfectwould + infinitivewould/could/might have + past participlecould / might

Second Conditional

If refrigerators weren’t common, we would eat and live differently.

Reality now: Refrigerators are common. The sentence imagines the opposite situation in the present and its possible result.

Third Conditional

If the compass hadn’t been invented, the Age of Discovery could have started much later.

Reality in the past: The compass was invented, and the Age of Discovery began when it did. Both events belong to a past that cannot be changed.

Mixed Conditional: past → present

If the printing press hadn’t been invented, books might still cost a fortune.

Reality: The printing press was invented in the past, so books are more accessible today.

Mixed Conditional: present → past

If there was no plumbing, living conditions might not have improved.

Reality: Plumbing exists as a general feature of modern life, and it contributed to improvements in the past.
