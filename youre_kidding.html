Discuss

Use the expressions you have just sorted.

  1. Choose several expressions. For each one, give a real situation in which you could genuinely use it. Say exactly how you would react.
  2. Which of these expressions would you actually use in a real English conversation? Which ones feel less natural to you?
  3. Do you react differently when speaking English and when speaking your first language? In what way?
Task 2

Watch and notice the reactions

Listen for short responses. Notice how quickly speakers react and what happens immediately after the reaction.
