5. Let’s discuss

Discuss the questions in pairs or small groups.

How popular is Halloween in your country?

Are these statements true about Halloween where you live?

Most people give out sweets to children, so shops sell a lot of them.
Some people like to go to the forest with friends, sit around a fire and tell spooky stories.
People enjoy watching scary films, so many cinemas show classics like Dracula or Psycho.
Adults rarely wear costumes for Halloween.
Young adults often throw Halloween parties with decorations and music.
Children dress up as witches or ghosts and go trick-or-treating from house to house.
What other things do people do for Halloween in your country?
