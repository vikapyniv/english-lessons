Meanings

A real future possibility

An alternative past that cannot be changed

A general fact or repeated result

An imaginary present or future

Possible sentences:

Zero: If drivers look at their phones, they become less focused on the road.

First: If the government introduces stricter speed limits, fewer people will be injured.

Second: If driving tests were more challenging, there would be better drivers on the road.

Third: If the cyclist had worn a helmet, he wouldn’t have suffered a serious head injury.
3

What is the reality?

Compare each conditional with the real situation. Then complete the mini-quiz.

PresentPast SimplewouldPerfect formFuture with will
Zero: If drivers use their phones, they become less focused.
→
Reality: This is a general, repeated result. It can happen whenever drivers use their phones.
First: If the government introduces stricter fines, fewer drivers will use their phones.
→
Possible future: The fines have not been introduced yet, but this is a realistic future result.
Second: If drivers were more patient, there would be fewer accidents.
→
Reality now: Drivers are not patient enough, and there are too many accidents.
Third: If the driver had slowed down, the accident wouldn’t have happened.
→
Reality in the past: The driver didn’t slow down. The accident happened.
