03 · Practical ideas

Beat the Scaries

Read the tips and discuss them.

Discuss:Have you tried any of these techniques?Would they work for you?When could you use them?Share a real example.
☑

List your tasks

Make a short to-do list for Monday on Sunday evening. When you have a plan, your brain feels calmer.

◉

Just breathe

Breathe in slowly and breathe out slowly. Do this for 2–3 minutes. It can help your body calm down.

↗

Do the opposite

If you want to hide in bed, do one small opposite thing: go for a walk, take a shower, or text a friend.

☀

Reframe Monday

Try to see Monday as a fresh start, not the end of the weekend. A new week = a new chance.

▣

Put the phone down

Turn off notifications on Sunday. If you read emails, your brain never really rests.

↝

Move your body

Even 20 minutes of walking or exercise lowers your stress hormones and makes you feel better.

✎

Write your worries

Write down what’s worrying you. Getting it out of your head and onto paper helps you feel lighter.

✦

Plan something fun

Plan one small thing to look forward to this week — a coffee, a film, a walk. It changes everything.
