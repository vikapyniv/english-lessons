Glimmers

1. Watch and discuss

Watch the video and complete each task when it pauses.

Discuss the questions

What do you think about the idea of glimmers?

Would you consider these experiences glimmers? Why or why not?

  • spotting a rainbow
  • the scent of a flower
  • a moment with a loved one

Have you tried anything similar, or would you like to try any of these things, to notice glimmers more often?

  • using a glimmer tracker
  • looking for joy in ordinary things
  • the first sip of coffee in the morning
  • organizing a desk
  • mindfulness and meditation
  • talking about glimmers

Are there times of the year when you notice glimmers more often? Does it also depend on the place?

Do you think schools, workplaces or cities could help people experience more glimmers? If yes, how?

2. How can places help us notice glimmers?

Look at the ideas below again and say:

  • Which ideas are likely to work?
  • Which ideas sound good but might not work?

Then think of how you would change these places to help people experience glimmers more often.

Schools

Teachers give students a few minutes at the end of each week to reflect on the little things that made them happy. Schools create unusual but enjoyable traditions so that students can hold on to happy memories about them long after graduation.

reflect on
think carefully about
hold on to
keep something important

Workplaces

Busy workplaces can be full of distractions and interruptions that trigger stress reactions. Quiet areas help employees relax and take their minds off problems. After such breaks they are more likely to notice things that can brighten up their day.

trigger
cause a reaction
take your mind off
help you stop thinking about something
brighten up your day
make you feel happier

Public spaces

It might seem that small changes to public spaces go unnoticed, but in fact, adding more benches and plants encourages people to slow down and savour the moment instead of rushing from place to place. Even mundane activities like waiting for a bus become more enjoyable if bus stops have comfortable seating or colourful artwork.

go unnoticed
fail to attract attention
savour
enjoy fully
mundane
ordinary and unexciting
