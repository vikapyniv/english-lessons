About a new product

Example: The Coffee Umbrella
Are you tired of rain getting in your coffee when you’re outside? The Coffee Umbrella is the perfect solution! It’s simple to use – put it in your coffee cup and adjust its height above your drink! Also works in snow or with cold drinks in hot weather! Get yours now!

Prepare a 30-second radio advert for your product.

Include at least one of the collocations in the box below.

Questions for the audience
  1. Do you think this product could exist in real life? Why/Why not?
  2. Would you use or buy this product yourself? Why/Why not?
  3. What problem does this product solve?
  4. What problems or disadvantages could this product have?
  5. What would you change or improve about the design?
