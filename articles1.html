Articles

Match the examples with the correct use of A/AN, THE or zero article.

Task:

A / AN

Indefinite article

Examples

I bought a book yesterday.
I ordered a coffee after lunch.
Tim sat down on a chair.
There is a hospital near the station.
She is a dentist.

Uses

New / first mention

We introduce one singular countable thing or person for the first time.

Any one / not specific

We mean one example from a larger group, not one particular known item.

Type / profession

We say what kind of person or thing someone or something is.

Correct answers — A / AN

New / first mention
I bought a book yesterday.
I ordered a coffee after lunch.
Any one / not specific
Tim sat down on a chair.
There is a hospital near the station.
Type / profession
She is a dentist.

THE

Definite article

Examples

I bought a book yesterday. The book is on my desk now.
Tim sat on the chair nearest the door.
I like the people I work with.
The coffee we had after dinner wasn’t very good.
I’d like to speak to the manager.
I’m going to the bank after work.
She has gone to the dentist.
I went to the university to meet a professor.
I left my phone on the bed.

Uses

Mentioned before

The noun was introduced earlier, so both speakers know which one is meant.

Specified / identified

Extra information identifies exactly which person or thing we mean.

Clear from context

The context makes it clear which person or thing is meant.

Specific place / object

We mean the physical place or object, not its usual institutional function.

Correct answers — THE

Mentioned before
I bought a book yesterday. The book is on my desk now.
Specified / identified
Tim sat on the chair nearest the door.
I like the people I work with.
The coffee we had after dinner wasn’t very good.
Clear from context
I’d like to speak to the manager.
I’m going to the bank after work.
She has gone to the dentist.
Specific place / object
I went to the university to meet a professor.
I left my phone on the bed.

Zero article Ø

No article

Examples

I love coffee.
I like working with people who say what they think.
Children go to school every day.
He was taken to hospital after the accident.
She wants to go to university next year.
I’m going to bed now.
What time do you finish work?

Uses

General meaning

Plural countable nouns and uncountable nouns used in a general sense.

Institution / normal purpose

school, university and hospital when we mean their usual function.

Fixed expressions

go to bed, go to work, be at work.

Correct answers — Zero article Ø

General meaning
I love coffee.
I like working with people who say what they think.
Institution / normal purpose
Children go to school every day.
He was taken to hospital after the accident.
She wants to go to university next year.
Fixed expressions
I’m going to bed now.
What time do you finish work?
