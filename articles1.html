Articles

A / AN

Indefinite article

Examples

I ordered a coffee after lunch.
Tim sat down on a chair.
There is a hospital near the station.
She is a dentist.

Uses

New / first mention

Any one / not specific

Type / profession

Correct answers — A / AN

New / first mention
I ordered a coffee after lunch.
Any one / not specific
Tim sat down on a chair.
There is a hospital near the station.
Type / profession
She is a dentist.

THE

Definite article

Examples

I like the people I work with.
The coffee we had after dinner wasn’t very good.
I’d like to speak to the manager.
I’m going to the bank after work.
She has gone to the dentist.
I went to the university to meet a professor.
I left my phone on the bed.

Uses

Specified / identified

Clear from context

Correct answers — THE

Specified / identified
I like the people I work with.
The coffee we had after dinner wasn’t very good.
I left my phone on the bed.
Clear from context
I’d like to speak to the manager.
I’m going to the bank after work.
She has gone to the dentist.
I went to the university to meet a professor.

Zero article Ø

No article

Examples

I love coffee.
I like working with people who say what they think.
Children go to school every day.
He was taken to hospital after the accident.
I’m going to bed now.
What time do you finish work?

Uses

General meaning

Main purpose of the place

Fixed expressions

Correct answers — Zero article Ø

General meaning
I love coffee.
I like working with people who say what they think.
Main purpose of the place
Children go to school every day.
He was taken to hospital after the accident.
Fixed expressions
I’m going to bed now.
What time do you finish work?
