Watch & discuss

If the video does not load here, open the interactive video on iSLCollective ↗.
After watching · discuss
  • Was the video informative? What else would you like to learn about the service?
  • Does understanding how BNPL loans work make people more likely or less likely to use the service? Why do you think so?
  • Is BNPL likely to become more popular in the near future? Why or why not?
  • Will BNPL replace credit cards? Why do you think so?
Think critically · give reasons
  • What are the possible advantages and disadvantages of offering BNPL from a merchant’s perspective?
  • Should BNPL be regulated? How? Or why might regulation be unnecessary?
  • Should BNPL be advertised? Why or why not?
  • Should BNPL providers and banks share customer data with each other? Explain your view.
